Biography

Simone Rocha

Born in Dublin on 11 September 1986, Simone Rocha is one of Ireland's most celebrated fashion designers. Of Irish-Chinese heritage — daughter of acclaimed designer John Rocha — she brings a rich duality to everything she creates.

Her design language is one of subversive femininity: Victorian silhouettes subverted with neoprene, pearls punctuated with industrial hardware, lace laid over raw structure. It is fashion that tells deeply human stories — of grief, nostalgia, girlhood, and strength.

Simone studied BA Fashion at the National College of Art and Design (NCAD) in Dublin in 2008, before completing her MA at Central Saint Martins in London in 2010. Her graduate collection launched her career at the highest level immediately.

In January 2024 she made history as only the sixth designer ever invited to be guest couturier for Jean Paul Gaultier in Paris.

Irish Roots

A Dublin Daughter

Ireland's wild coastlines, Celtic mythology, and the rich traditions of Irish craft are woven into every collection. From the Little Ark of Kilbaha to the grandeur of Goldsmiths' Hall — Simone carries Dublin with her always.

Creative Collaborations

🏷️

H&M

Simone Rocha became the first Irish designer to create a full collection for H&M, bringing her signature pearl-and-lace aesthetic to a global audience in a sell-out collaboration.

Designer Collaboration • 2021
✨

Jean Paul Gaultier Haute Couture

As guest couturier — only the 6th designer ever to receive this honour — Simone created an Haute Couture collection in Paris merging her Irish sensibility with Gaultier's transgressive French heritage.

Guest Couturier • January 2024
🏔️

Moncler Genius

As creative director of the Moncler Genius AW18 collection in Milan, Simone reimagined the iconic Moncler puffer jacket — adding bows, pearls and lace to outerwear in an extraordinary reinterpretation.

Creative Director • AW18, Milan
🐊

Crocs

Simone Rocha's SS24 partnership with Crocs produced pearl and crystal-embellished platform Crocs that sold out within hours — challenging conventions of luxury and utility simultaneously.

Design Collaboration • SS24
🎨

Genieve Figgis

For SS25, Irish-born artist Genieve Figgis — known for dreamlike oil paintings — collaborated with Simone Rocha to have original artworks printed directly onto garments, blurring the boundary between fashion and fine art.

Artist Collaboration • SS25
